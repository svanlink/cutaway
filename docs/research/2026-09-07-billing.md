# The Invoice quarter: what to charge, and what Cutaway may say about it
*Billing department · 2026-09-07 · builds on `2026-09-05-billing-invoicing.md`. Primary sources re-verified except where noted.*

## 1. Two products hide in one sentence

**(a) What do I bill THIS client for THIS job.** A settled, defensible number for finished work. Cutaway owns this outright — it holds the sessions, the rate in force when each was worked, the typed corrections, the period.

**(b) What should my rate BE.** Backward-looking: what last quarter *actually* earned per hour once unbilled time is counted. Cutaway owns one number here: **realised rate** = invoiced ÷ *all* hours attributable, tracked and typed, billed and written off. On a fixed-budget project it is the only honest reading.

    realised_rate = Σ invoiced / Σ (billable_hours + written_off_hours)

Cutaway refuses market benchmarks, "you should charge X", target-income calculators, income forecasts and tax provisioning: advice, not arithmetic, and an app with no peer data cannot produce them honestly. One line per closed project — *CHF 4'500 fixed / 51.3 h = CHF 87.72 realised, list rate CHF 120* — and the owner draws the conclusion.

## 2. The invoice

**Document model.** `Invoice` is a frozen snapshot, never a view over live sessions.

```
Invoice: number, issueDate, dueDate, periodStart, periodEnd, currency,
         taxMode, taxRate, supplierBlock, clientBlock, lines, subtotal,
         taxAmount, total, status(.draft/.issued/.paid/.void), qrReference?
InvoiceLine: kind(.time/.dayRate/.minimum/.manual/.deposit), date?,
             description, quantity, unit, unitPrice, amount, adjustedHours
```

Snapshot, because a rate edit, day edit or time-zone move must not change a document already sent. The quarter's most important decision.

**Numbering.** `INV-YYYY-NNNN`, sequential per calendar year, gapless, allocated at *issue*. Drafts carry no number.

**The page.** Supplier block (name, street, postcode/town, country, UID + "MWST" only if registered) · client block · number, issue date, supply period · one line per day (date, description, hours, rate, amount), grouped by week past 400 lines · subtotal · tax line or tax-mode note · total · terms · QR part for CHF/EUR.

**Disclosing typed hours without looking uncertain.** Do not annotate rows — a per-row asterisk reads as doubt about the work. The day line shows hours plainly; one footnote below the totals states the fact once —

> *2.5 of the 51.3 hours billed were entered manually rather than measured; time is recorded only while the editing application is in front, so measured time under-states actual work.*

That argues *for* the supplier. Printed only when `Σ adjustedHours > 0`; the CSV keeps the per-day `adjusted_hours` column.

**Locking.** On issue every session in the period gets `invoiceID` and turns read-only: edit, delete and day-adjust refuse — "Invoiced on INV-2026-0007 — void that invoice first". Voiding keeps the number and releases the sessions. A running timer's open span is excluded, not truncated.

## 3. Swiss and EU law — primary sources

- **Required fields — MWSTG Art. 26 para. 2**: supplier name and location *with the VAT number under which they are registered*; recipient name and location; date or period of supply where it differs from the invoice date; nature, object and extent of the supply; the consideration; the **tax rate and tax amount** (rate alone if tax-inclusive). [Fedlex SR 641.20](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2009/615/en) — re-verified 2026-09-07.
- **Threshold — Art. 10 para. 2 let. a**: exempt below **CHF 100 000** worldwide turnover from non-exempt supplies in one year.
- **The trap — MWSTG Art. 27**: someone not in the register **may not show tax**, and anyone who does *owes the tax stated* unless the invoice is corrected. So a non-registered Sebastian prints **no VAT line, no rate, no "0 % VAT", no UID** — a bare total and at most "Not subject to Swiss VAT (Art. 10 para. 2 let. a MWSTG)". Cutaway must make a tax line impossible while `registered == false`. [Fedlex, ibid.] Registered rates: 8.1 / 2.6 / 3.8 % ([ESTV](https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/vat-rates-switzerland)).
- **EU B2B**: place of supply is the customer's country and the customer accounts for the tax (Directive 2006/112/EC Art. 44, Art. 196); the invoice carries both VAT identification numbers and the literal words **"Reverse charge"** (Art. 226 (3), (4), (11a)) — [EUR-Lex consolidated 2025-01-01](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02006L0112-20250101). *EUR-Lex blocked automated fetch this round; wording carried from the 2026-09-05 verification.* Two cautions: as a third-country supplier Sebastian is not bound by the Directive's invoicing rules — the mention serves the customer; and with no network there is no VIES check, so the app must never imply a typed VAT ID was validated. USD/COP: no VAT line, no mention.
- **CHF 0.05 rounding — do not do it.** No ESTV or Fedlex text mandates it; the ESTV practice publications produced nothing this round. Five-Rappen rounding is a *cash* convention (1- and 2-Rappen coins are withdrawn). Invoices settle by transfer at the centime and the QR amount field permits 0.01, so rounding to 0.05 only makes the printed total and the QR amount disagree. **Overrules recommendation #4 of the predecessor report.**
- **QR-bill — IG v2.3 in force since 21 Nov 2025; v2.4 lands 14 Nov 2026, v2.3 valid until Nov 2027** ([SIX](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/banking-services/payment-standardization/standards/qr-bill.html)). From [IG v2.4](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/banking-services/standardization/qr-bill/ig-qr-bill-v2.4-en.pdf): "Version 2.4 does not result in any technical adjustments for invoicing in Swiss francs (CHF). For invoicing in euros (EUR), only the combination IBAN/SCOR reference and IBAN/unstructured message is possible." Also: address type **"S" — structured only** (combined address removed); QR reference (26 digits + mod-10) needs a **QR-IBAN, CHF only**; Creditor Reference (ISO 11649, RF + mod-97-10) needs a **normal IBAN, CHF or EUR**; CHF/EUR only, 0.01–999 999 999.99, always two decimals. **Build to v2.4 now** — it lands inside this quarter and costs nothing extra for CHF.

## 4. What editors bill — ruling on each

| Model | Ruling | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Day rate (10 h in post)** | **Model.** | `dayRate`, `dayHours` (10), `otMultiplier` (1.5). A day ≤ 10 h bills one day-rate line; beyond, `(h − 10) × dayRate/dayHours × 1.5`. A 4.2 h day bills one day, printed "1 day (4.2 h worked)" — hours are never inflated to reach a day. |
| **Hourly, 2–4 h minimum** | **Model.** | **Per day**, never per session, as its own line: "Minimum applied — 21 Mar, 1.1 h worked, 2.0 h billed". Worked and booked both visible. |
| **Deposit** | **Model** as a manual negative line with its own date — "Deposit received DD.MM.YYYY". Never negative hours. |
| **Included revision rounds** | **Refuse.** Cutaway sees applications, not deliverables; inferring rounds from timeline gaps is a guess printed as fact. One manual line ("Revision round 3 — beyond the two included, 3.5 h") is the honest surface. |
| **Kill fee** | **Model** as a manual line only. No percentage calculator — 25 % vs 50 % is a contract term Cutaway never read. |
| **Non-billable admin** | **Model** as a flag, never a line. `billable: false` keeps a session out of every invoice total and inside the realised-rate denominator — the only way (b) is answerable. |
| **Retainers, late fees, expenses** | **Refuse.** Ledgers and accruing interest need state Cutaway must keep correct forever. |

## 5. Arithmetic invariants that must be pinned by a test

Already pinned: rows sum to total; last cumulative row equals the total; `budget_remaining` agrees with the total; a raise does not reprice past work; the blended `effectiveRate` reconciles `hours × rate` with `earned`; a period excludes outside days. **Missing, in priority order:**

1. **One rounding mode everywhere.** `round2` is half-away-from-zero; `BillingCurrency.format` is `NumberFormatter`'s default **half-even**. Pin `format(round2(x)) == format(x)`. Fails today (§6).
2. **Screen equals document.** Stats footer, Σ visible Stats day rows and the CSV/invoice total are the same string for any project and period — i.e. *round once at the printed precision, then sum*, asserted on both surfaces rather than only inside the CSV. Untested and false today.
3. **Multi-currency minor units.** COP has zero decimals; `format` truncates to whole pesos while the CSV prints `%.2f` — COP 1'395'000.50 shows *COP 1.395.000* on screen, `1395000.50` in the file. Pin: granularity is a property of the currency (2 for CHF/EUR/USD, 0 for COP) and every surface uses it.
4. **A rate change mid-project, including on an edited day.** `SessionStore.setActiveSeconds` stamps `project.hourlyRate` — the rate *now*. Correcting a January day in March bills January hours at March's rate; after a raise, that **over-bills**. Pin: an adjustment carries the rate of the day it corrects.
5. **Midnight and time zone.** Days group with `Calendar.current` at read time; move the Mac Zurich → Bogotá and late-evening sessions regroup onto the previous day, changing a month total after the invoice was sent. Pin: an issued invoice's total is time-zone independent (snapshots fix it; the test must still exist), and a zero-span adjustment anchored at 23:59:59 never migrates to the next day.
6. **A fixed-budget project that overruns.** `total_earned` runs past the budget today — 48 h × 120 = **CHF 5'760** against a CHF 4'500 budget, `budget_remaining` = −1'260. An invoice built from it bills CHF 1'260 nobody agreed to. Pin: bill `min(accrued, budget)`; print the overrun as "not billed", never as a line.
7. **Typed time survives in both directions.** The shrink path mutates tracked sessions downward and flags nothing, so `adjusted_hours` reads 0.00 on a hand-corrected day — benign for the client, corrosive for the record. Pin: any hand-set day is marked, up or down.

## 6. Verified in the code: no, the totals do not agree

**Question:** does a CSV-derived invoice-period total always equal the sum of the day rows shown in Stats, including on days with adjusted sessions?

**Method:** compiled `CSVExporter.swift` and `BillingEngine.swift` unmodified against a stub `DayTotal`; compared `export`'s `total_earned` against `StatsView`'s two totals over 200 000 generated months (15–22 days, 1–11 h/day, CHF 45–150).

**Result — two failures, one exoneration.**

- `StatsView.swift:100` and `:240` compute `days.reduce(0) { $0 + $1.earned }` — the **unrounded** sum, rounded once at display; `CSVExporter` accumulates `cumEarned += round2(earned)`, the sum of **printed** values. They disagree in **74.8 %** of months.
- Σ of the *visible* Stats day rows disagrees with the CSV total in **40.9 %** of months: `round2` (half-away) and `format` (half-even) split on ties, and ties are common — `earned = seconds/3600 × rate` lands on an exact half-rappen whenever `seconds × rate ≡ 18 (mod 36)`. Smallest real case: **CHF 45/h, 3 610 s → CSV `45.13`, Stats `CHF 45.12`** — and the CSV is the *higher*, so it also breaks the under-billing rule.
- Adjusted sessions add no *third* failure — `adjustedSeconds` sits inside `activeSeconds` and `earned` — but they carry the rate bug (invariant 4) and the invisible-shrink bug (invariant 7).

| 04.03 | 5.94 | 120.00 | 713.33 |
| 05.03 | 5.95 | 120.00 | 713.93 |
| 09.03 | 8.67 | 120.00 | 1'040.83 |
| 11.03 | 4.96 | 120.00 | 594.90 |

CSV `total_earned` = **CHF 6'211.39** (Σ printed rows). Stats shows **CHF 6'211.40** (round of 6 211.400000000001). One rappen, same eight days, same app — the invoice inherits whichever surface it is built from.

Registered: 6'211.39 + 8.1 % = 503.12 → **CHF 6'714.51**; QR amount `6714.51`, **not** rounded to 6'714.50. Non-registered: **CHF 6'211.39**, no tax line at all (Art. 27). Creditor Reference from `INV-2026-0007` → **RF63 20260007** (mod-97-10 check = 1), normal IBAN, valid CHF and EUR under IG v2.4.

**Fix, in order:** give `round2` and `format` one shared currency-aware rounding function; make `StatsView` sum the rounded day values; add invariant 2 as a test reading both surfaces.

## Arbitration 2026-09-07: the invoice data model

*Ruling. Billing wins the substance; Architecture wins the sequencing and one structural point.*

### 1. Storage: snapshot, with references for provenance only

`Invoice` is a frozen snapshot. Architecture's duplication objection is noted and
overruled — an invoice is a document, not a query, and a view over live sessions cannot
survive a rate edit, a day edit or a Zurich→Bogotá move. Architecture's real win: the
snapshot must not be the *only* record of where a figure came from.

```
@Model Invoice   // every printed field is a COPY
  number, statusRaw(.draft/.issued/.paid/.void), issueDate, dueDate,
  periodStart, periodEnd, currencyRaw, taxModeRaw, taxRate,
  supplierBlock, clientBlock: String,        // copies, never a Client ref
  subtotal, taxAmount, total: Double,
  timeZoneIdentifier: String,                // the zone the days were grouped in
  qrReference: String?,
  @Relationship(deleteRule: .cascade, inverse: \InvoiceLine.invoice) lines: [InvoiceLine]

@Model InvoiceLine
  kindRaw(.time/.dayRate/.minimum/.manual/.deposit), day: Date?, text: String,
  quantity, unit, unitPrice, amount, adjustedHours: Double,
  sessionUIDs: [String]                      // provenance, NOT a relationship

WorkSession  += uid: UUID = UUID(), invoiceNumber: String = ""
```

`sessionUIDs` as strings, not a SwiftData relationship: a relationship's inverse pulls
live sessions back into the invoice — the exact coupling Architecture fears — and drags
invoices into every session fetch. `invoiceNumber` is the lock, denormalised so the
guard is checkable without loading the invoice. Non-empty = locked. Every figure stays
traceable (doctrine) and every figure is a copy (Billing).

### 2. What "locked" means

**Refuse:** `setActiveSeconds` on a day holding a locked session; deleting a locked
session; deleting or reassigning a project with locked sessions; any rate edit that
would reprice locked work. Message names the number.
**Allow with warning:** new work recorded inside an invoiced period — it lands
unlocked and bills next period, never silently folded back (under-bill bias); a
forward-only rate change.
**Correction path: void-and-reissue.** Not a credit note — that needs an accruing
cross-document balance, the same forever-correct state Billing §4 already refused for
retainers and late fees. Not an unlock — an issued document that can silently disagree
with the store destroys the property this whole ruling buys. Voiding keeps the number
(printed VOID, never reused, sequence stays gapless), clears `invoiceNumber` on its
sessions, and the correction then happens in the ordinary editing path.

### 3. Schema versioning lands first. Blocking.

`SchemaV1` (Project, WorkSession, `versionIdentifier 1.0.0`) + an empty
`SchemaMigrationPlan` ship in their own commit; `Invoice`/`InvoiceLine` are SchemaV2.
What breaks otherwise: adding two entities to `ModelContainer(for:)` changes the store's
implicit schema identity with no declared origin, so a later `SchemaV1` has no version
to migrate *from*. The only path left is `SessionStore()` throwing into the in-memory
fallback — `storeIsEphemeral` plus a banner someone misses while a render runs, and a
day of billing into RAM. ~25 lines, no second chance.

### 4. Currency

**A session does not stamp currency.** Its money is `activeSeconds × hourlyRate` and the
rate is already stamped; currency belongs to the agreement, not the minute, and
per-session currency invites a mixed-currency day no honest total can add. The
**invoice** stamps it.
Changing a project's currency: refused outright once any session is locked. While
nothing is invoiced, allowed with an explicit confirmation that **converts nothing** —
past amounts are reinterpreted, not translated. Conversion needs a rate Cutaway cannot
fetch (no network) and a date it cannot defend.
Minor units are a property of the currency (COP 0, CHF/EUR/USD 2) in one shared
function used by `round2`, `format` and the CSV.
