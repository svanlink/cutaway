# Billing for freelance editors: from tracked time to a professional invoice
*Generated 2026-09-05 | Sources: 54 | Confidence: high on regulations and product patterns (primary texts, vendor docs); medium on editor practice (US-weighted forums) and complaints (aggregators, no raw App Store scrape). Numbered citations; list under Sources.*

## Executive summary
Editors bill in chunks: 10-hour day rates with 1.5×/2× overtime, hourly with a 2–4 h minimum, 30–50 % deposits, 2–3 included revision rounds, kill fees. Cutaway has only hourly-or-budget. Serious trackers share five primitives: billable flag, per-entry rounding that keeps the raw value, an "unbilled" view, one-click invoice with detailed/grouped/summary lines, and locking entries once invoiced. A Swiss invoice needs the Art. 26 MWSTG fields and, to look native, a QR-bill payment part (IG v2.3: structured address, CHF/EUR, two decimals). No Swift QR-bill library exists, but the payload is a short text spec and SwiftUI `ImageRenderer` → `CGContext` yields a vector PDF with zero dependencies. Top complaints — export-and-rebuild loops, totals that disagree, no invoiced/paid state, pricing shocks — all argue for a local, boring, correct invoice inside Cutaway.

## 1. What editors bill and how
- **Time-based is the default** — "the safest in almost every situation"; flat rate makes you "a post producer responsible for an outcome". Long-form weekly, commercial daily or hourly; rough cuts on day rate, revisions hourly [1](https://www.reddit.com/r/editors/wiki/index/ratesv2/).
- **Day = 10 h in post, OT after.** "A day rate is generally 10 hours in Post. Anything beyond that reverts to a predetermined hourly rate" [3](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/day-rates-how-do-they-workae/); 1.5× after 8 h, 2× after 12, sixth day 1.5× [1](https://www.reddit.com/r/editors/wiki/index/ratesv2/); IATSE Local 700: 8 h minimum, 1.5× after 8, 2× after 12–14 [2](https://therateguide.com/video-editor-day-rate).
- **Minimums.** "4 hour minimum is standard" for hourly gigs [4](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/la-editorshourly-or-day-rateae/); half days "up to 4 hours", full day beyond [5](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/freelance-billing-time-for-a-day-and-weekly-rate/page/2/).
- **Rounding** is a tool setting, not an editor norm — no editor source names 6/15-minute increments. The honest practice is a plain-language log ("color match across 6 interview clips, 2.0 hrs") so hours aren't disputed [6](https://www.waffleinvoice.com/blog/how-to-invoice-as-a-video-editor).
- **Revisions:** 2–3 rounds included, extras per round or hourly, invoiced before work resumes [7](https://pixflow.net/blog/freelance-video-editing-contract-template/), [8](https://www.markstudios.com/blog/how-to-price-video-production-services-2026).
- **Deposits, kill fees, retainers:** 30–50 % upfront; kill fee 25 % pre-start, 50 % or work-to-date mid-project; retainers with monthly caps and 30-day notice [7](https://pixflow.net/blog/freelance-video-editing-contract-template/); shoot cancellations 50–75 % inside a week [10](https://delivvo.io/blog/freelance-contracts-for-video-projects-2026).
- **Expenses:** templates default to "Client will reimburse the Editor's expenses", Net 15, 1.5 %/month late fee [9](https://www.hellobonsai.com/contract-template/video-editing).

## 3. Swiss/EU invoice requirements and QR-bill
- **Mandatory fields (Art. 26 para. 2 VAT Act):** supplier name/location with VAT-registration note and number; recipient name/location; supply date/period if it differs from invoice date; nature, object, extent; consideration; rate and tax amount (rate alone if tax-inclusive) [29](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2009/615/en).
- **Threshold:** liable at CHF 100 000 worldwide taxable turnover; below, exempt unless opted in — then no VAT line at all [30](https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/vat-tax-liability). Rates 8.1 / 2.6 / 3.8 % [31](https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/vat-rates-switzerland).
- **EU B2B client:** VAT payable by the customer for Art. 44 services from a non-established supplier (Art. 196); invoice needs a sequential number, both VAT IDs and the literal mention "Reverse charge" (Art. 226 (2)(3)(4)(11a)) [35](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02006L0112-20250101). USD/COP clients: no VAT, no mention.
- **CHF 0.05:** cash rounds to 5 Rappen; non-cash may settle any amount [36](https://help.comarch.com/pos/index.php/knowledge-base/rounding-of-cash-payments-for-the-swiss-market/). Formula ×20, round, ÷20 [37](https://www.kingbill.com/de/blog/die-schweizer-rappenrundung.html); ESTV reportedly accepts commercial rounding per line or on the total if consistent (secondary) [38](https://hilfe.flexbuero.ch/article/1105/). Convention: total to 0.05.
- **QR-bill:** IG v2.3 since 21 Nov 2025; v2.4 from 14 Nov 2026 changes nothing for CHF, restricts EUR to IBAN+SCOR or IBAN+message [32](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/banking-services/payment-standardization/standards/qr-bill.html), [34](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/banking-services/standardization/qr-bill/ig-qr-bill-v2.4-en.pdf). Rules: amount 0.01–999 999 999.99, two decimals, or blank; CHF/EUR only; QRR needs a QR-IBAN, SCOR/NON a normal IBAN; structured addresses only; message + billing info ≤140 chars; payment part 148×105 mm right of a 62×105 mm receipt on the A4 lower edge; Arial/Frutiger/Helvetica/Liberation Sans 6–10 pt [33](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/banking-services/standardization/qr-bill/ig-qr-bill-v2.3-en.pdf). Swico S1 embeds invoice number, date, UID and VAT rates in billing info [39](https://www.codecrete.net/qrbill-javadoc/net/codecrete/qrbill/generator/SwicoBillInformation.html).
- **Libraries:** none in Swift. Reference ports: [41](https://github.com/manuelbl/SwissQRBill) (Java, 182★, Jul 2026, MIT) and [40](https://github.com/schoero/swissqrbill) (TS, 242★, v4.3.0 Feb 2026, MIT). Only Swift precedent: [42](https://github.com/jorisnoo/Harvie), a 6★ SwiftUI macOS app (Aug 2026) adding SCOR QR-bills to Harvest invoices — small job, not a dependency.

## 4. Building invoices locally on macOS
- **ImageRenderer → PDF**, zero dependencies: `renderer.render { size, draw in … CGContext(consumer:mediaBox:) … beginPDFPage/endPDFPage/closePDF }`; text stays vector [46](https://www.hackingwithswift.com/quick-start/swiftui/how-to-render-a-swiftui-view-to-a-pdf). Multi-page: mutate `renderer.content` per page on one context [48](https://swiftcrafted.dev/article/swiftui-imagerenderer-view-to-uiimage-pdf-guide). Print via `PDFDocument.printOperation(for:scalingMode:autoRotate:)` + print entitlement [47](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/763719).
- **QR code:** [44](https://github.com/dagronf/QRCode) (718★, MIT, Jun 2026) renders CGPath/PDF with logo overlay (Swiss cross) and all EC levels; the payload is a newline-separated string per the IG.
- **Pure-Swift invoice DSLs** are immature: [43](https://github.com/Swiftly-Developed/Swiftly-PDFKit) (1★, Aug 2026) and [45](https://github.com/megabyte0x/invoicegen) (0★, local-first, `INV-2026-0001.pdf`). Borrow the numbering, skip the dependency. Template = a SwiftUI `InvoicePageView`; DE/FR/IT/EN labels via `String(localized:)`, money via the existing formatter.

## 5. What users complain about
1. **Tracker stops at the report.** "Every billing cycle I export to a spreadsheet… build the invoice in FreshBooks manually. That's 30 minutes of admin per client" [49](https://www.plutio.com/freelancer-magazine/still-using-toggl); "Export to CSV, format manually, send as PDF. Every. Week." [55](https://dev.to/wubingwx/harvest-raised-prices-10x-heres-what-freelancers-actually-need-from-an-invoice-tool-1phd).
2. **Numbers that don't reconcile.** Report vs invoice rounding [19](https://clockify.me/help/projects/invoicing); exports "introduce rounding errors, missed entries" [50](https://www.plutio.com/freelancer-magazine/still-using-clockify); "put 2.5, which is wrong because it means 2 hours 50 minutes" [52](https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/harvest/reviews/).
3. **No invoiced/paid state.** Toggl can't mark entries paid [16](https://support.toggl.com/en-us/article/creating-invoices-in-toggl-track-1irsvlv/); "not super user friendly to keep track of time taken off of a retainer… the PDF invoice isn't super great" [53](https://www.softwareadvice.com/hr/clockify-profile/reviews/).
4. **Lost billable time** from silent timer failures and sync loss [54](https://trackeyflow.com/reviews/harvest/).
5. **Pricing and lock-in shocks.** Harvest renewals up to 1 500 %, "daylight robbery" [51](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/clyq011414eo); Clockify refund denials, 3-hour account deletion [50](https://www.plutio.com/freelancer-magazine/still-using-clockify). 

## Recommendations for Cutaway (ranked)
**Under-billing honesty, explicit:** Cutaway never inflates time. Rounding is opt-in, defaults *nearest* or *down*, never silently up; raw seconds stay in the record and CSV beside the rounded value; manual edits stay flagged `adjusted_hours` on the invoice; minimums and day rates appear as "booked, not worked" lines, never as padded hours.

1. **Invoice PDF from a period (M).** `Invoice` record: `INV-YYYY-NNNN` [45](https://github.com/megabyte0x/invoicegen), issue/due date, currency, client and Art. 26 supplier blocks [29](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2009/615/en), lines Detailed / by day / Summary [21](https://support.freshbooks.com/hc/en-us/articles/227602308-How-do-I-generate-an-invoice), rendered via ImageRenderer [46](https://www.hackingwithswift.com/quick-start/swiftui/how-to-render-a-swiftui-view-to-a-pdf). Data source = existing period picker and CSV summary. Done when Preview shows selectable text.
2. **Unbilled view + lock (S).** `billingStatus` (unbilled/invoiced/paid) on sessions, unbilled total per project in the popover, lock on invoice [14](https://support.getharvest.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048687171-Detailed-time-and-detailed-expense-reports), [26](https://timingapp.com/help/time-entries). 
3. **Rounding per project (S).** Off / nearest / down at 6 or 15 min, per session before totalling; both columns reported [11](https://support.getharvest.com/hc/en-us/articles/360053116772-How-does-time-rounding-work). One assert: Σ rounded == invoice total.
4. **Client entity with tax mode (M).** Name, address, country, VAT ID, currency, rate, terms. Tax mode from country + owner registration: CH registered → 8.1 % + UID [31](https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/vat-rates-switzerland); CH below threshold → no VAT line [30](https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/vat-tax-liability); EU B2B → 0 %, "Reverse charge", both VAT IDs [35](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02006L0112-20250101); other → none. CHF totals to 0.05 [37](https://www.kingbill.com/de/blog/die-schweizer-rappenrundung.html).
5. **QR-bill on CHF/EUR invoices (M).** IBAN/QR-IBAN + structured address in settings; SCOR reference from the invoice number as Harvie does [42](https://github.com/jorisnoo/Harvie); payload per IG 2.3, QR via dagronf/QRCode with Swiss cross, 148×105 mm part at page bottom, Helvetica 6–10 pt [33](https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/banking-services/standardization/qr-bill/ig-qr-bill-v2.3-en.pdf). Validate with a banking app.
6. **Day rate and minimum units (M).** Per project: hourly / day (N h, OT multiplier after) / budget; 2 or 4 h minimum as its own "minimum applied" line, worked vs booked visible [4](https://creativecow.net/forums/thread/la-editorshourly-or-day-rateae/), [1](https://www.reddit.com/r/editors/wiki/index/ratesv2/).
7. **Manual lines: deposit, revision round, expense, kill fee (S).** Free-form Service/Product lines [15](https://support.getharvest.com/hc/en-us/articles/360048686431-Configuring-invoice-and-estimate-settings); deposit as a negative line [7](https://pixflow.net/blog/freelance-video-editing-contract-template/). No expense module — YAGNI.
8. **Skip:** Stripe links, reminders, retainer ledgers, client portal — they need a server or account, the lock-in users hate [51](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/clyq011414eo).
