# The Cutaway team

Six standing departments. Each is a real agent definition in `.claude/agents/`,
re-summonable in any future session with its full charter intact — the doctrine,
the constraints, and the history it must not repeat.

| Department | File | Owns | Summon it when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Doctrine** | `cutaway-doctrine.md` | The six rules, the keep/kill calls, what the app refuses to be | Deciding what to build or delete |
| **Architecture** | `cutaway-architect.md` | Swift/macOS structure, launch time, CPU, memory, energy, SwiftData safety | Structural change, performance, before a big feature |
| **Billing** | `cutaway-billing.md` | Rates, invoices, Swiss/EU VAT, QR-bill, rounding, every number a client could dispute | Anything that turns time into money |
| **Detection** | `cutaway-detection.md` | Resolve and Adobe detection, permissions, idle and presence, energy of watching | Anything about when the clock runs |
| **Interface** | `cutaway-interface.md` | Pill, panel, Stats, Settings, every editing surface, dark-only system, accessibility | Anything a human sees or clicks |
| **Quality** | `cutaway-quality.md` | Tests, silent failures, data safety, backups, the release gate | Before any release, after any money/time/store change |

## The six rules every department serves

1. The timer is a consequence, not a button.
2. Every ambiguity resolves toward under-billing.
3. Local forever — no account, no network, no telemetry.
4. The menu bar is the app; Stats and Settings are rooms you visit.
5. Resolve-first, not a generic freelancer tracker.
6. Corrections are allowed, but they leave a scar.

## How the team works

1. **Round 1 — research.** Every department investigates its own area against the
   code and the open web, and writes `docs/research/<date>-<department>.md`.
   Each one is told to name where it expects another department to disagree.
2. **Round 2 — cross-examination.** The contested points go back to the
   departments that disagree. They argue it out with evidence; nothing is
   settled by seniority.
3. **Round 3 — synthesis.** One plan: what gets built, what gets deleted, in
   what order, with the owner's decisions isolated as questions.

A department may refuse work that violates the six rules. That refusal outranks
convenience, and it outranks me.
